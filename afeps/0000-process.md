# AFEP-0000: Advanced Fabrics Enhancement Proposal Process

- Status: Final
- Type: Process
- Created: 2026-09-12

## Purpose

AFEPs are the public decision record for Advanced Fabrics. A proposal is required for a new stable API, compatibility change, security-boundary change, provider protocol change, or governance change.

## Types and states

Types are Standards, Process and Informational. States are Draft, Provisional, Accepted, Final, Rejected, Withdrawn and Superseded. AFEP numbers contain four digits and are allocated when a pull request is opened.

## Required content

Every Standards AFEP defines motivation, goals and non-goals, normative behavior, security considerations, compatibility, conformance assertions, alternatives and rollout. Normative requirements use MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD and MAY as defined by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174.

## Review and decision

Discussion occurs in a public pull request. Standards proposals remain open for review for at least 14 calendar days. During bootstrap, the Bootstrap Lead records a reasoned decision after maintainer review. After the Maintainer Council forms, ordinary proposals require a simple majority; governance changes require two-thirds approval. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed.

Accepted proposals are not Final until their reference implementation, conformance tests and public documentation ship. Breaking changes to a GA contract require a new major version.
